How to practise

Mode A — Listen & recreate

Play the hidden rhythm, then use the note palette to enter the same rhythm. Notes and rests fill the bar from left to right. Press Check answer when the bar is complete.

Mode B — Read & identify

Compare the notation options and play the audio examples. Select the one that matches the rhythm shown, then check your answer.

Settings

Difficulty changes the note values used. Triplets and syncopation can be switched on or off. Tempo controls playback speed.

This is a learning prototype. Its notation is a simplified single-line rhythm staff, not a full engraving engine.
